# Closed-test invite

The message to send the twelve. Opt-in link, verified live on 2026-09-01:
**https://play.google.com/apps/testing/pt.jogarbumi.app**

Three things decide whether this works, and all three are in the message below:

1. The link only works for an address on the mailing list attached to the *alpha* track
   (`Teste fechado 2026-08`, 12 addresses). Someone opening it from a different Google account
   sees an error, so the person has to be signed into Play with the address you invited.
2. Pressing *Become a tester* is not enough — Play then has to be opened and the app installed.
   Google counts testers who opted in **and** have it installed.
3. The count is read per day. An uninstall part-way through drops it below twelve and restarts
   the fourteen days, which is why the message asks for two weeks rather than a try.

Play can take a few hours to show the app after opting in; installing from the same link's
*Download it on Google Play* is the reliable path.

## English

> Subject: Can you test Bumi for 2 weeks?
>
> Hi,
>
> Bumi is finished, and one thing stands between it and Google Play: Google requires 12 people to
> test the app for 14 continuous days before it will let it launch. You are on the list — all
> that is missing is you joining.
>
> Two steps, on your Android phone:
>
> 1. Open this link and tap *Become a tester*:
>    https://play.google.com/apps/testing/pt.jogarbumi.app
> 2. On the same page, tap *Download it on Google Play* and install.
>
> Two things that matter:
>
> - You must be signed in to Google Play with the email address this message was sent to. Any
>   other account and the link errors out.
> - **Leave it installed for 14 days.** Uninstalling part-way resets Google's count to zero and
>   the two weeks start again — for everyone, not just you. You do not have to play daily; it
>   just has to stay installed.
>
> If the app does not show up straight away, give it an hour or two and open the link again —
> Google is slow to propagate.
>
> Anything broken, or any opinion at all: reply here or write to suporte@jogarbumi.pt.
>
> Thank you — without these 12 people the app does not ship.
